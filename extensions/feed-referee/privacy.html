Privacy & limits.

What leaves your browser

When you add your own TypeSafe API key and enable Feed Referee, the visible text of supported posts across X (including Home, profiles, post pages, search, lists and bookmarks), plus visible quoted text, is sent directly to api.typesafe.ai for Jev to judge. This can include protected posts you have permission to view. TypeSafe processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy; the extension does not control their retention.

No requests go to Jev Experiments servers. There is no analytics, advertising, telemetry, or remote executable code.

What is not collected

No passwords, cookies, DMs, account tokens, browsing history, images, videos, linked-page contents, or other tabs' contents. Display names and handles are not separate API inputs, but names or other personal information written in a post are part of its text.

Local storage

Your key is kept in extension session storage by default. “Remember key” stores it on this device until you remove it or uninstall. Chrome extension storage is not an encrypted vault. It is not synced to Chrome accounts. The key is not accessible to X or the extension's content script.

Settings and daily request/token counters stay local. A bounded judgment cache stores hashes of post IDs/text and probabilities, not raw text, for up to seven days. Use “Clear judgment cache” in the popup to delete it. Removing the key does not clear cached judgments; uninstalling removes extension storage.

Judgments, not facts

Labels are model estimates and can be wrong. Ragebait, promotional intent, AI-like writing patterns (AI Slop), and engagement farming are independent questions. Text alone cannot reliably establish authorship; an AI Slop label is a style estimate, not proof of AI use. The detail panel describes the rubric, not a generated explanation or verified evidence.

Only rendered posts in the main content column are processed. DMs, composers, drafts, account/settings pages and dialog content are excluded. Media-only, very short and over-limit posts are skipped. Posts with Show more are judged using the text X has loaded, with a truncation caveat. Expanding a post can trigger an updated judgment. Quotes supply context only and receive no separate badge or request. Missing context, irony, quotes, and non-English text may reduce accuracy. Images, videos, and linked pages are never analyzed.

Control & costs

The extension starts paused. Enable, pause, choose optional collapsing, reveal any collapsed post, and set a daily request cap in the popup. API requests are charged to your own TypeSafe account. The cap counts attempts including failures/retries, across tabs, resetting at midnight UTC. It is not a dollar spending limit or a guarantee of the provider's billing.

Pausing aborts pending requests where possible and removes extension UI. Already-sent requests may still be billed. Failures leave posts visible, with bounded retries and cooldowns. Layout changes on X may require an extension update.
